Almost every university now agrees that work experience belongs inside the degree. The harder question is one of capacity: how do you offer authentic, real-world experience to hundreds or thousands of students without the model collapsing under its own administrative weight? The honest answer is that the traditional placement model does not scale, and the institutions widening access fastest are the ones that have accepted this and designed around it.
